    What you are looking at

    Every reasoning step of every agent run is embedded and projected with PCA onto the ground. Height is how often agents reasoned there, so consensus paths rise into ridges and the lesson's failure region sinks into a pit. Past the data the ground continues as dunes without end, and the camera follows the swarm. Each beetle is one agent in its company's color, walking its real steps in order and leaving its path behind it. A beetle that reaches the step SubQ blamed flips onto its back and stays there.

    The ground is printed as halftone dots: higher ground gets bigger, denser dots. Each beetle's track is pressed into the ground itself, printed with the same dots, and fades over about twenty seconds. The beetles are pixel sprites on a layer above, so nothing ever covers them. The camera fits every beetle in view; drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and re-centre to hand it back. Live run replays the recorded run as a stream, subtask by subtask: agents deploy, hidden tests decide, a flag is planted where a beetle fell, and later beetles steer around it.
