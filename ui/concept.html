What you are looking at

Every reasoning step of every agent run is embedded, then projected with PCA. Height is how often agents reasoned there, so consensus paths become ridges and the lesson's failure region is a pit. Each beetle is one agent in its company's color. It walks its real steps, in order, and leaves a trail that fades. A beetle that reaches the step SubQ blamed flips onto its back and stays there.

Reduced motion is on in your system, so beetles stand still and trails are drawn in full.
