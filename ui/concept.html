What you are looking at

Every reasoning step of every agent run is embedded and projected with PCA onto the ground. Height is how often agents reasoned there, so consensus paths rise into ridges and the lesson's failure region sinks into a pit. Past the data the ground continues as dunes without end, and the camera follows the swarm. Each beetle is one agent in its company's color, walking its real steps in order and leaving its path behind it. A beetle that reaches the step SubQ blamed flips onto its back and stays there.

The ground is printed as halftone dots: higher ground gets bigger, denser dots. Each beetle's track is pressed into the ground itself, so it is printed with the same dots and stays. The beetles are pixel sprites on a layer above, so nothing ever covers them. Click an agent in the legend to follow it; live run polls the store and turns new runs into beetles.
