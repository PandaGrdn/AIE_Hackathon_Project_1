What you are looking at

Every reasoning step of every agent run is embedded and projected with PCA onto the ground plane. Height is how often agents reasoned there, so consensus paths rise into ridges and the lesson's failure region sinks into a pit. Past the data the ground continues as dunes without end, and the camera follows the swarm. Each beetle is one agent in its company's color, walking its real steps in order. A beetle that reaches the step SubQ blamed flips onto its back and stays there.

The scene is drawn in 3D, then every pixel becomes one halftone dot: brighter surfaces get bigger dots, dark ground gets almost none. Click an agent in the legend to follow it.
